10-Day Reset

Recover Your Life
in 10 Days.

You don't need to completely reinvent yourself. You need to remove the noise, rebuild your habits and regain control of the things you stopped controlling.

The Concept

This isn't a miracle.

Ten days will not magically solve every problem in your life. But ten focused days can be enough to interrupt a destructive routine and establish a completely different rhythm.

It's not a detox. It's a recalibration.

Your body already has systems responsible for processing and eliminating waste. The goal of this reset is not to "flush toxins" from your body with a special product or extreme diet. The goal is to clean up your routine: your sleep, environment, food choices, movement, screen habits, organization and daily discipline.

What You're Resetting

Clean the areas that control your day.

Your environment and habits constantly influence what you do. Instead of trying to change everything at once, use ten days to attack the biggest sources of chaos.

Sleep

Establish a consistent sleep and wake schedule and remove unnecessary stimulation before bed.

Nutrition

Reduce ultra-processed foods and build meals around nutritious, minimally processed ingredients.

Movement

Walk, train and spend more time moving instead of spending the entire day sitting.

Environment

Clean your room, desk, computer and the spaces you interact with every day.

Screen Time

Remove unnecessary scrolling and reclaim attention that is constantly being consumed by your phone.

Discipline

Stop negotiating with every small task. Create simple rules and follow them consistently.

The 10-Day Reset

Ten days. One reset.

The objective is not perfection. The objective is to spend ten days behaving differently long enough to establish a new baseline.

DAY 01

Clean Your Environment

Your environment constantly reminds you what to do. Start by removing unnecessary friction and visual chaos.

Clean your room.
Clean your desk/workspace.
Throw away obvious trash and unnecessary clutter.
Organize the place where you train.
Prepare tomorrow's clothes and essentials.
DAY 02

Reset Your Sleep

Stop treating sleep like whatever happens after you finish scrolling. Give it a place in your schedule.

Choose a consistent wake-up time.
Avoid unnecessary screen stimulation before bed.
Keep your sleeping environment comfortable and dark.
Get natural light during the day.
DAY 03

Clean Up Your Nutrition

Don't look for a magical detox drink. Start removing the foods and habits that make your diet harder to control.

Prioritize minimally processed foods.
Build meals around nutritious protein sources.
Include fruits and vegetables that you tolerate and enjoy.
Reduce excessive sugary drinks and highly processed snacks.
Drink enough fluids throughout the day.
DAY 04

Move Again

Movement does not have to mean destroying yourself in the gym. Start rebuilding daily activity.

Take a walk.
Train if your body is ready.
Break up long periods of sitting.
Spend time outside.
DAY 05

Digital Detox

Your attention is one of your most valuable resources. Stop giving it away automatically.

Delete or hide unnecessary distractions.
Turn off non-essential notifications.
Create periods with no social media.
Do one important task before entertainment.
DAY 06

Rebuild Your Morning

The first hour of your day should not automatically belong to your phone.

Wake up at your planned time.
Get natural light.
Hydrate.
Move your body.
Decide your most important task for the day.
DAY 07

Take Back Your Time

A chaotic schedule makes good habits difficult. Today, identify where your time actually goes.

Write down your main daily activities.
Identify your biggest time leaks.
Remove one unnecessary activity.
Reserve time for training, work and recovery.
DAY 08

Train Your Discipline

Discipline becomes easier when you stop making every action a debate.

Choose 3 non-negotiable habits.
Complete them regardless of motivation.
Finish one task you have been postponing.
Avoid unnecessary "I'll do it later" decisions.
DAY 09

Build Your System

Motivation disappears. Systems remain. Turn the habits from the previous eight days into a repeatable routine.

Define your normal wake-up time.
Define your training schedule.
Define your screen-time boundaries.
Define your basic nutrition rules.
Define your nightly routine.
DAY 10

Lock It In

The reset ends today. The system does not. Decide which habits are staying.

Review the previous nine days.
Identify what improved your life the most.
Remove habits you don't want to return to.
Keep your most important daily habits.
Create your next 30-day challenge.
Daily Baseline

Your daily reset checklist.

These are simple behaviors. Their power comes from repetition, not complexity.

Wake up around your planned time.
Get some natural daylight.
Drink enough fluids.
Eat nutritious, minimally processed food.
Move your body.
Complete your most important task.
Limit unnecessary scrolling.
Keep your environment organized.
Prepare for tomorrow before going to sleep.
No Extreme Detox

Don't turn a reset into another problem.

You don't need starvation, dangerous fasting, detox supplements, juice-only diets or extreme exercise to reset your routine.

No starvation.

A reset should make your routine more sustainable, not deprive your body of necessary nutrition.

No miracle detox products.

Your body already has organs and biological systems that process and eliminate waste.

No punishment workouts.

Exercise should build your capacity, not be used as punishment for what you ate or did yesterday.

No perfection obsession.

Missing one habit does not destroy the entire reset. Return to the system at the next opportunity.

The system is what comes next.
