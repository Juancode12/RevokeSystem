Improve Your Skin

Clearer, healthier-looking skin can dramatically change your appearance.

Your skin is one of the first things people notice, but improving it doesn't require an unnecessarily complicated routine.

Start With The Fundamentals

01 — Nutrition

Diet doesn't cause every case of acne, and acne has multiple causes. However, dietary patterns can influence acne in some people.

02 — Skincare

A simple routine built around cleansing, moisturizing and sun protection can be a better foundation than using dozens of products.

03 — Facial Massage

Facial massage may temporarily reduce the appearance of puffiness and help with facial relaxation.

Important:

If acne is severe, painful, persistent, or causing scarring, seek help from a dermatologist or qualified healthcare professional.

Your Bite & Facial Development

Your teeth, bite and jaw are connected to the structure and function of your mouth.

During childhood and adolescence, dental development and jaw growth are particularly important.

If you have concerns about your bite, jaw position, crowding or facial development, a dentist or orthodontist can evaluate your individual situation.

Don't DIY your jaw.

Online trends can exaggerate what techniques such as "mewing" can accomplish. Natural tongue posture is part of normal oral function, but it should not be treated as a guaranteed method for dramatically reshaping an adult jaw.
