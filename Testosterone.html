What Causes Low Testosterone?

Low testosterone levels in modern men are primarily caused by multiple interconnected factors including poor diet high in processed foods, sedentary lifestyles, chronic stress, sleep deprivation, environmental toxins (endocrine disruptors in plastics and chemicals), excessive alcohol consumption, and obesity. These modern lifestyle factors disrupt the body's natural hormone production, leading to a significant decline in testosterone levels observed across Western countries over the past decades.
